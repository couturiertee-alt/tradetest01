In this video I'll explain my EMA crossover strategy for Bitcoin on the 1-hour chart.

I use a 20-period EMA and a 50-period EMA. When the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA I go long. When the fast EMA crosses below the slow EMA I exit or go short.

For risk management: stop loss is 2% below entry. Take profit target is 2:1 risk/reward ratio.
I size my positions at 5% of total account equity per trade.

This works best on BTC/USDT. I only trade in the direction of the daily trend.
I also use RSI(14) to filter out overbought entries — I won't long if RSI is above 70.
